⚙️ Live Trading Rules · 25 USDT account

ALERT-ONLY
  • Run ONE engine at a time. S1 or S2 — never both on the same 25 USDT account. S2 live automatically skips every order while the S1 bot is running; stop S1 first to let S2 trade.
  • Best plan only. A signal trades live only on high conviction — long ≥ 85 / short ≤ 15 on the 0–100 meter (stricter than the 70/30 alert threshold). No BTC-regime filter.
  • Liquid pairs only. Just the top 150 most-traded USDT perps are eligible; everything thinner stays alert-only.
  • Fixed small size. 4× leverage · 1.5 USDT margin/trade (max 2.3) · max 10 concurrent positions — total exposure can never exceed the account.
  • Every position is bracketed. A full stop-loss (ATR, capped 4%) + a single 100% take-profit at 2R rest on Binance, so the trade is protected even if the scanner is offline. No partials at this size.
  • Master switch. Live orders need STRATEGY2_LIVE=true and the global LIVE_TRADING=true. With either off, signals are logged as a dry-run — no real order.

Currently alert-only. To go live: stop the S1 bot, set STRATEGY2_LIVE=true in app/.env, then start the scanner with ./run_strategy2.sh.
