🐺 Wolf Scanner Strategy Rules

Overview — How a Trade Happens
Wolf Scanner scans the top {{ scan_limit }} Binance Futures pairs on the 1-hour chart for market breadth, but only trades the most liquid top {{ trade_limit }} — the rest are shown with a Watch only tag and never generate a trade. The flow is:
  1. Scan every hour → score each coin with 5 base "lights" + SMC bonus lights.
  2. Qualify only the strongest setups (5+ lights) that also pass every filter below.
  3. Queue a resting entry at a planned price — the trade waits until price actually touches that entry (no chasing).
  4. Fill & manage — once touched, the position opens with a fixed Stop-Loss and a two-stage Take-Profit (50% / 50%).
Every trade risks a defined amount (R = entry → stop distance) so reward is always measured in multiples of risk.
Conviction System — 5 Base Lights + SMC Bonus
L1: Stochastic RSI (StochRSI)
Applies a stochastic formula to RSI-14 values, producing K and D lines that oscillate 0–100. Parameters: RSI(14) → Stoch(14) → K smooth(3) → D smooth(3).

LONG signal: K < 20 (oversold zone) or K crosses above D while K < 35.
SHORT signal: K > 80 (overbought zone) or K crosses below D while K > 65.

Unlike raw RSI which rarely hits 90/10 on altcoin charts, StochRSI oscillates the full 0–100 range frequently — detecting momentum reversals within RSI rather than absolute extremes. Replaces the old RSI ≤10 / ≥90 threshold.
L2: Strategy Score (≥8 / 10)
A 0–10 bull/bear score built from RSI recovery/rejection, volume surges, OBV trend, Bollinger-band breakouts and support/resistance breaks. A breakout only scores if volume ≥ 1.5× its 20-bar average. The light fires only when the score reaches 8 or higher.
L3: EMA Momentum / Trend
Price must be above (LONG) or below (SHORT) the EMA50, confirming the short-term trend agrees with the trade direction.
L4: TSI Signal
True Strength Index (TSI) must show bullish (LONG) or bearish (SHORT) momentum confirmation.
L5: MACD Signal
MACD must show bullish (LONG) or bearish (SHORT) crossover or momentum confirmation.
SMC Bonus Lights (can push total above 5)
  • +2 Liquidity Sweep: Price swept a prior swing high/low (stop-hunt) before reversing — highest-conviction SMC signal.
  • +1 CHoCH (Change of Character): Market structure broke in favor of the trade direction, confirming a trend shift.
  • +1 or +2 Order Block Grade: +1 for a standard OB in confluence, +2 for a premium-grade OB (strong reaction history, clean impulse).
The SMC bonus is capped at +3, so maximum effective lights = 8 (5 base + 3 SMC). A 6+ light setup means every base condition is met plus strong SMC confirmation.
Trade Plan — Risk-Based 1 : 2
R = the risk = distance from entry to stop-loss. Targets are set as multiples of R, so the reward-to-risk is a true 1 : 2 on every coin, no matter its volatility.
  • Entry: A planned price set an ATR-based offset below (LONG) / above (SHORT) the current price. The setup is queued and only fills when price actually touches that level (a resting limit, not a market chase).
  • Stop-Loss (SL): ATR × 1.5 beyond the nearest swing high/low (structure), capped at 4% max. This distance defines 1R.
  • TP1 = 1R (1× the stop distance): close 50% of the position to lock in profit.
  • Runner (remaining 50%): closes at TP2 = 2R, with the stop moved to breakeven after TP1 (so the runner can't turn into a loss). A trailing-runner variant was backtested and dropped — it added no edge and only made the live exchange behave differently from the dashboard. This exact bracket (TP1 1R, TP2 2R, breakeven stop) is what runs live, so the simulation and a real account match.
  • Minimum conviction: ≥ 5 effective lights (both directions).
    • Backtesting (90-trade sample) proved 4-light setups have a negative edge (34.6% win, −0.27R) while 5+ lights are clearly profitable (+0.24R). Raising the floor to 5 roughly tripled the strategy's expectancy.
    • At least 2 of the 5 base lights must be real — SMC bonus lights alone can never qualify a trade.
Entry Conditions — Symmetric (LONG = SHORT)
Every rule below is a mirror image for both directions, so the bot takes longs and shorts with equal opportunity — no more all-long bias when the market turns down.
  • 🟠 BTC regime ("don't fight Bitcoin"): alts amplify BTC's move, so the bot blocks LONGs while BTC is in a bear regime and SHORTs while BTC is bullish (BTC vs a rising/falling 1H EMA50). This is the biggest win-rate lever — it stops you fighting the whole market.
  • Conviction lights: ≥ 5 effective lights for both LONG and SHORT, with ≥ 2 real base lights (SMC bonus alone can't qualify). Raised from 4 — backtests proved the 4-light tier had a negative edge.
  • RSI guard (avoid exhausted extremes):
    • LONG: blocked when RSI ≥ 80 (don't buy the blow-off top).
    • SHORT: blocked when RSI ≤ 20 (don't short the capitulation bottom).
  • 4H trend bias: LONG only when price > 4H EMA50; SHORT only when price < 4H EMA50.
  • EMA50 / EMA200 alignment in the trade direction (1h).
  • Momentum aligned: RSI rising for LONG, falling for SHORT.
  • Volume gate: signal candle volume ≥ 1.5× the recent average.
  • SMC zone (symmetric): the swing range is split discount (lower 40%) · equilibrium (middle 20%) · premium (upper 40%). LONG is blocked in premium, SHORT is blocked in discount. Entering at the ideal zone (LONG in discount, SHORT in premium) earns a +1 bonus light — rewarded, not required.
  • Not over-extended: price must still be below TP1 (LONG) / above TP1 (SHORT).
  • Data quality: ≥ 60 closed candles + a fresh latest candle.
  • No active same-direction trade already open on that symbol.
Risk Protection & Cooldowns
  • Partial take-profit: 50% closed at TP1 (1R), 50% rides to TP2 (2R) — same in dry-run and live.
  • After a stop-loss (SL): that symbol + direction is on a 3-hour cooldown — no re-entry, because freshly-invalidated setups tend to chop. (The opposite direction is still allowed.)
  • After a take-profit (TP): no cooldown — the coin can re-qualify on the very next scan (the "not over-extended" rule still stops you chasing the same move).
  • Queue hygiene: if a queued setup's target prints before the entry fills, the queue is auto-cancelled (the move already happened — chasing it loses). A duplicate pending entry won't be re-recorded within 2 hours.
Daily Circuit Breaker
The bot halts all new trades for the rest of the day (Taiwan time, resets at midnight) when either limit is hit:
  • {{ circuit.max_losses if circuit else 5 }} losing trades in one day, or
  • {{ circuit.max_drawdown_pct if circuit else -5.0 }}% cumulative loss in one day (sum of losing trades only — wins don't offset it).
Only new entries are blocked — trades already open keep running their TP/SL. The banner at the top of the dashboard shows today's live status.
Cadence & Controls
  • Scans the top {{ scan_limit }} pairs by volume on Binance Futures for a diverse market view; only the most liquid top {{ trade_limit }} are tradeable (others carry a Watch only tag).
  • New-signal scan every hour (on the 1h candle close); queued + open trades are re-checked every 1 minute (with 1-minute candle wicks) for fast, accurate TP/SL fills.
  • Manual close: close an open trade from the Performance page — it won't count as a win or loss.
  • Remove queued: drop a waiting setup from the Performance page before it fills.
Live vs Simulation — Know the Difference
The dashboard always runs the simulation tracker. A live Binance account (when enabled) follows the same plan with two intentional differences in how the stop is managed:
Behaviour Simulation (dashboard) Live account
Entry / TP1 / TP2 Queued entry, 50% at 1R, 50% at 2R Resting LIMIT + two reduce-only TP orders (50% / 50%) — identical
Stop-loss before TP1 Full position at ATR/structure stop Full position STOP_MARKET on the exchange — identical (protects you even if the bot is offline)
Runner after TP1 TP2 at 2R, stop moved to breakeven Identical — reduce-only TP2 order + breakeven STOP_MARKET — matches
Sizing Tracks % return only Real margin = fixed USDT × leverage × conviction scale
Bottom line: the live account mirrors the dashboard end-to-end — same entries, same 50/50 partial TPs (TP1 1R, TP2 2R), exchange-side full stop, and breakeven after TP1. The only difference is real position sizing (margin × leverage). So whatever PnL you see on the web is what a connected account would have done. Live trading is OFF by default (dry-run) until you add API keys and flip LIVE_TRADING=true.
